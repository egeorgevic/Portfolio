About

I'm an engineer who designs, which means the sites I build hold up under the hood as well as on the screen.

Engineering and Management graduate from the University of Exeter with a first-class degree. I currently work as a software engineer building UI components, parametric features and 3D-modelling software for HVAC systems, and I've been freelancing as a web designer since 2023.

That mix is the point. I can sit in a meeting with a CFO, understand what the business actually needs, sketch the flow, design the interface and ship it — without handing off between three people who each understand a third of the problem.
